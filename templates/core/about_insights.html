How AI works in LeaderLodge

Computed by LeaderLodge. Explained by AI.

The idea

LeaderLodge measures performance from your game history. Cloud language models turn those measurements into readable summaries. The model does not calculate ranks or invent scores. It interprets data LeaderLodge already computed.

Two ways to ask

Contextual insights
On rank detail, leaderboards, Hall of Fame, matchup, and game detail pages, open the Insights panel beside the data you are viewing. Choose a preset (Strengths, Overview, Dynamics, and others) or ask a short custom question about that snapshot.
Ask the Lodge
A dedicated page for natural-language questions scoped to your group and game type. The model requests validated queries; Python runs them and returns facts before the answer is written.

What we send to AI providers

  • Computed stats: rankings, placements, trends, and group summaries
  • Player display names from your group roster
  • Your question or preset choice

We do not send your password or full account details. We do not let the model run arbitrary database queries or raw SQL.

Models and control

Insights use server-side API keys only. Signed-in users can pick among a small set of low-cost models. Answers are cached when appropriate, and rate limits can be enabled site-wide.

Every insight panel includes a disclaimer: AI summary. Rankings are computed by LeaderLodge.
