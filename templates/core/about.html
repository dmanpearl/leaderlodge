About LeaderLodge

LeaderLodge helps game groups record results, track standings, and celebrate leaders over time.

What it does

  • Record games with scores, dates, locations, and player counts
  • Rank players per game type within each group using field-weighted finish points
  • Browse leaderboards, player detail, matchup views, and Hall of Fame history
  • Organize members, guests, and group permissions

Built for game night

LeaderLodge is mobile-friendly and designed for recurring groups: dominos, backgammon, mah jongg, progressive rummy, and other game types your lodge supports. Rankings stay consistent because they are computed in Python from your recorded games, not guessed.

AI insights

LeaderLodge can explain your stats in plain language. Rankings are computed by LeaderLodge; AI narrates what the numbers mean.
