About LeaderLodge

LeaderLodge helps groups of people who play games together record results, track standings, discover hidden insights, explain your stats, and celebrate leaders over time.

What it does

  • Record games with scores, dates, locations, and player counts
  • Rank players per game type within each group
  • Browse leaderboards, player detail, matchup views, and Hall of Fame history
  • Organize members, guests, and group permissions

Built for game night

LeaderLodge is mobile-friendly and designed for recurring groups: Dominos, Backgammon, Progressive Rummy, Mah Jongg, Pickleball, Gin Rummy, Cribbage, Phase 10, Darts, Cornhole, and more. Rankings are computed from your recorded games.
